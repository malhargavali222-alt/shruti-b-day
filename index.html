Dear Shruti,

Happy Birthday! Today the world officially gets a little brighter, a little louder, and a lot more entertaining, all because you were born. 🎂

I have called you Pikachu, Dhole Madam and Dok Khau so many times that your real name sounds like a formal announcement. But every nickname means the same thing: you are someone I am really glad to have in my life.

I have watched you work towards your architecture diploma with so much dedication. You draw lines on paper like they are already part of tomorrow's skyline, and I truly believe one day they will be.

I also know the big dream: you will be so successful that people will need an appointment just to meet you. When that day comes, remember that I knew you before the waiting list. I will be there with a coffee in hand.

Thank you for the laughs, the chaos, the endless talking, and for making ordinary days feel like small celebrations. Keep dreaming big, keep drinking your coffee, and keep being exactly who you are.

Your friend,
Malhar
